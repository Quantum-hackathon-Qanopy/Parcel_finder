Where quantum could change this

A projection. Not built or measured.

Estimating at scale. Amplitude estimation needs about 1/ε runs where sampling needs 1/ε². For ±1% that is roughly 2,000 instead of 98,000. It pays off when each run is expensive, such as a stochastic deforestation model over every pixel of a project, for thousands of projects, refreshed weekly.

Choosing comparison parcels. Picking the best 50 of thousands of candidates under many constraints is a search that explodes in size. Classical solvers win at every size we can run today. Quantum optimisation is a candidate beyond them, not a proven one.

What it takes. Error-corrected hardware: gate errors near 10-6, about 3,000× better than today's 0.3%. Until then the classical result is what we publish.

The payoff: auditable estimates for every credit, continuously, at a cost that scales to a whole market and not a handful of projects.
